    The Discovery Pipeline

    How Idea Enhancer Evaluates Ideas

    A deterministic hybrid retrieval architecture designed to eliminate blind spots without fabricating similarity scores.

    1

    Describe Idea

    Submit your technical abstract detailing hardware, architecture, machine learning models, and application goals.

    2

    Dual Retrieval

    Simultaneous keyword matching via BM25 and semantic embedding retrieval in ChromaDB using bge-small-en-v1.5.

    3

    Rank Fusion

    Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) combines sparse and dense rankings mathematically without inventing arbitrary similarity percentages.

    4

    Evidence & Angle

    Inspect shared technical concepts and verification tags to understand prior art and discover opportunities to differentiate.

    ⚖️

    Guiding Principle & Scope Boundaries

    Idea Enhancer is a research copilot for student engineers, not a plagiarism detector. It cannot prove universal originality or catalog all worldwide projects. It retrieves traceable matches from indexed academic and historical databases.

    RRF scores are fusion ranking values, never similarity percentages. Our mission is to help you explore past engineering work with confidence and clarity.
